Jar Biome: Privacy Policy

Developer: ThisIsExperience · Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Jar Biome: Idle Ecosystem (com.thisisexperience.jarbiome)

Summary

Jar Biome has no accounts and no sign-in. Your jars, Genes and settings are saved on your device. The game uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and Google AdMob to show optional reward videos and occasional ads between moments, and Google Play Billing for one-time purchases. It contains no separate analytics service. We do not sell personal data.

Your ad choice

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks once whether ads may be personalised, before any ad can show. Until you answer, ads are not personalised. You can change the choice at any time in Settings → Personalised ads. Elsewhere you can turn personalised ads off in the same place. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Reward videos only play when you tap a button that shows the reward.

Children

Jar Biome is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Retention and deletion

Settings → Start over deletes your saved progress on the device, and uninstalling the game deletes it too. We hold no data about you on our own servers. Ad data is handled by the ad networks under their policies below; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. For any privacy question, contact us at the address below.
