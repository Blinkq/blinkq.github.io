Privacy Policy — Domino Pips: Logic Puzzles

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Domino Pips: Logic Puzzles ("the app", package com.thisisexperience.dominopips) is published by ThisIsExperience ("we").

Summary. The app has no accounts and no in-app purchases. It is free and shows ads, served through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. It uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played (for example which puzzles need the most hints), so we can improve it. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal content.

Analytics: what is collected. Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, collects:

  • App activity: game events such as starting, solving or leaving a puzzle, its difficulty, time taken, hints and checks used, flawless solves, daily-puzzle streaks and streak freezes, tutorial steps, screens viewed, settings changed (sound, music, vibration, timer, show broken rules, numbers instead of pips, colour-blind patterns, left-handed buttons, daily reminder, theme, language, personalised ads), whether a rating prompt was shown, and which ad placements were used, with the ad revenue estimate.
  • Device and app information: a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase, the device model, operating-system version, app version, language, approximate country (derived by Google from the IP address, which is not stored by us), and the time and length of sessions.
  • Advertising ID: only if you allow personalised ads (see below), to measure which of our own ads brought you to the game.

The data is sent over an encrypted connection to Google and is used only by us to understand and improve the game and to measure our own advertising. It is not used for ad personalisation or remarketing. Google processes it as our service provider under the Google Analytics terms and the Firebase data processing terms. Event-level analytics data is kept for 2 months (Google Analytics' standard retention) and then deleted automatically; only aggregated, non-identifying reports are kept longer.

Ads. Ads are shown by Unity Ads, through the Unity LevelPlay mediation service (Unity Technologies). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these services receive your device's advertising ID and identifiers they create for your device, the IP address (from which they estimate your approximate location, such as your country or city), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), app diagnostics, and how you interact with ads. They process it under Unity's privacy policy.

  • Personalised ads. If you allow it, ads can be chosen for you based on your advertising ID and activity. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks before the first ad and shows non-personalised ads until you agree. Everywhere else ads are personalised unless you turn it off. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Personalised ads. Turning it off also tells the ad services not to "sell" or "share" your data, as US state privacy laws use those words.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Watching a rewarded ad (for an extra hint) is always optional.

What is stored on your device. Your progress and settings (solved puzzles, best times, daily streak and freezes, unfinished boards, hints used today, sound, theme, language, layout and ad preferences) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. We cannot access them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them; Settings › Reset progress erases your progress too.

Permissions. The app uses the internet for the analytics and ads described above, the advertising ID permission for ads, vibration for optional light haptics (Settings › Vibration), and notifications only if you turn on the daily reminder.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices. Turn personalised ads off in Settings, reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and reset the app's analytics identifier by clearing the app's data or reinstalling it. To ask a question, or to request deletion of data linked to your device, contact us at the address below. For data held by Unity, you can also contact Unity through its privacy policy.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
