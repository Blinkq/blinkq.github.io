Euchre: Bower Club Privacy Policy

Developer: ThisIsExperience · Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Euchre: Bower Club (com.thisisexperience.euchre) · Applies to version 1.0

Summary

Euchre: Bower Club has no accounts and no sign-in, and you play against the computer on your own device. Your games, Table Notes, stamps and settings are saved on your device. The game uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and Google AdMob to show optional reward videos and occasional ads between hands, and Google Play Billing for one-time purchases. We do not sell personal data.

Your ad choice

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks once whether ads may be personalised, before any ad can show. Until you answer, ads are not personalised. You can change the choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Elsewhere you can turn personalised ads off in the same place. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Reward videos only play when you tap a button that shows the reward.

Purchases

Purchases are one-time and never random. Settings → Privacy & ad choices → Restore purchases brings them back from your Google Play account on a new device.

Children

Euchre: Bower Club is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress on the device. We do not run our own servers and keep no data about you. Data the ad networks hold is covered by their policies below; resetting your advertising ID unlinks it. For any privacy question, contact us at the address below.
