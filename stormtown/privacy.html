Stormtown: Privacy Policy

Developer: ThisIsExperience · Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Stormtown: Idle Incremental (com.thisisexperience.stormtown)

Summary

Stormtown has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress is saved on your device. The game uses Unity LevelPlay (with Unity Ads) to show optional reward videos and occasional ads between storms, and Google Play Billing for one-time purchases. It does not use an analytics service. We do not sell personal data.

Data the game handles

DataWho receives itWhy
Game progress (sparks, upgrades, settings)Stays on your deviceSaving your game
Advertising ID (when allowed), device information, IP address, ad interactionsUnity LevelPlay and its ad network (Unity Ads)Showing ads, measuring ad delivery, preventing fraud
Purchase history (product and order ID)Google Play (the payment is processed by Google, we never see card details)Delivering and restoring one-time purchases

Your ad choice

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks once whether ads may be personalised, before any ad can show. Until you answer, ads are not personalised. You can change the choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choice. Elsewhere you can turn personalised ads off in the same place. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Daily reminders

If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled by your device's notification system. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

Stormtown is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress. We do not keep data about you on our own servers. For data held by Unity, you can contact Unity through its privacy policy; you can also contact us with any question.

Third-party policies

  • Google (Google Play)
  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads)

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when a later version adds an analytics service, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date before that version is released.
